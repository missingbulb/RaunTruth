# Pitch, documentary cut

**Tone:** Calm and lab-real: macro footage, a warm piano and strings bed, a narrator who sounds like a respected colleague. Silence is allowed; the clicks open it.
**Voice:** `en_US-lessac-medium` at length scale 1.08 (the draft; a cast voice replaces it).

The scenes, what each conveys and its pictures are the [brief's](2-the-loop.md); this cut gives each moment its
hold, camera, voice and sound. Camera and sound cues are the backticked tokens, read by the animatic; the rest is
direction.
